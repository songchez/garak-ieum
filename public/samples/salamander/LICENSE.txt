Accurate-Salamander Grand Piano V6.2e (remaster of Salamander Grand Piano V3)
by Alexander Holm (Salamander Grand Piano V3); remaster by Chisato Yamauchi, Accurate-Salamander Project
Source: https://www.ir.isas.jaxa.jp/~cyamauch/AccurateSalamander/

Licensed under CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Changes made for this project: 30 notes × 4 of 16 velocity layers (v3, v7, v11, v15) and 88 key-release samples selected from the 48 kHz/24-bit FLAC bank; trimmed with per-register length caps and fade-outs, one common gain for the whole set, encoded to Opus 96 kbps (webm) and AAC 128 kbps (m4a)
